Legal Services for Nonprofits & Community Organizations

  • Bylaws, board policies and conflict of interest procedures
  • Grant application review and strategy
  • Grant agreement review and negotiation
  • Compliance consultation
  • Financial management, procurement and personnel policies
  • Real estate acquisition, leasing and disposition
  • Program design and funding strategy

  • Ballot initiative and referendum drafting and filing

  • Policy platform drafting and advocacy strategy
  • Training and technical assistance

Legal Services for Developers

  • Capital stack structuring and gap financing strategy
  • Housing development consultation
  • Low Income Housing, Historic, New Markets and other Tax Credits
  • Layering HOME, CDBG and other public sources
  • Tax Increment Financing, abatement and redevelopment agreements
  • Purchase and sale agreements, ground leases and easements
  • Entity formation, joint venture and operating agreements
  • Davis-Bacon, Section 3, URA and fair housing compliance

  • Construction contracts, disbursing agreements and draw requests
  • Closing coordination

  • Policy design consultation
  • Uniform Guidance interpretation and application
  • Single audit readiness, findings and corrective action plans
  • Consolidated Plans, Annual Action Plans and Consolidated Annual Performance and Evaluation Reports
  • Environmental review
  • Subrecipient agreements, monitoring and grant closeout
  • Compliance manuals, policies and procedures
  • Legislative drafting: ordinances, resolutions and appropriations
  • Charter amendment and ballot measure drafting
  • Charter, civil service, procurement and other good government reforms

  • Testimony and public hearing support
